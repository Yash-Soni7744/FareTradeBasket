About Us

Fare Tradebasket India Pvt. Ltd. is a dedicated agribusiness enterprise connecting grassroots production with wider markets. We aim to uplift farming communities by replacing inefficiencies with transparency and creating sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To transform agricultural and fisheries value chains through transparency, innovation, and farmer empowerment, ensuring fair returns for producers and quality for consumers.

Our Vision

To build a resilient, equitable, and sustainable agricultural ecosystem where every Indian farmer thrives on the principles of fair trade.
